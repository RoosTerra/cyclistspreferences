# Understanding cycling route choice behaviour through street-level images and discrete choice models
Code and data to reproduce the paper submitted to Transportation Research Part A: Policy and Practice. A preprint of the paper is available [here](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5473360) (not been peer 
reviewed yet).

This study examines cyclist route choice behaviour using a stated choice experiment with real-world street-level images, analysing preferences for cycling infrastructure and environment. We apply discrete choice models and computer vision to map alignment between Rotterdam's cycling network and cyclist preferences, and simulate the effects of infrastructure upgrades.
